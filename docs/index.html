Maria Iontseva · DPhil, University of Oxford

Phenomena

Type Operation Texts
Revelation a deity or the “dream boy” (svapnamāṇavaka) answers in the dream: outcome of the rite; past, present, future; hidden treasure Niśvāsa Guhyasūtra 3; Svacchanda 3–4; Mṛgendra Kriyā VII; Brahmayāmala 34, 55; MVT 23; JY Ṣ3; Kubjikāmata 23; Kumārikākhaṇḍa 33; Gandharvatantra 26; Dīkṣāpaddhati; Yoginītantra 7; Śrītattvacintāmaṇi 5
Empowerment mantra or initiation received in a dream; a text revealed between waking and sleep Mahārthamañjarī 12, 71; Śaktisaṃgama 3.180–181; Tantracintāmaṇi; Tantrasaṃgrahadhātuvāda
Magic another person’s sleep is the target: send a dream, destroy sleep (vaśīkaraṇa), force awakening, sleep a raised corpse JY Ṣ4 yakṣiṇī; Vārāhītantra, Parātantra, Tridaśaḍāmara; Indrajālavidyāsaṃgraha; Gaurīkañculikā; Vīṇāśikha; Puraścaryārṇava II
Transformation the dream state itself: awareness in dream; the guru enters the sleeping disciple Śivasūtra I.9–11; Spandakārikā Vibhūti 1–3; Tantrāloka 10, 15, 28–29; Dīkṣāpaddhati
Verification dream as test before initiation (adhivāsana), before taking a liṅga stone, a temple site, a yantra 9 of 16 MPhil texts; Kālottara; Mohacūḍottara; Hāhārāva; Vārāhītantra 2

svapnamāṇavaka / svapnādhipati
Rite kuśa bed, one garment, right side; morning report to the guru
Verse namaḥ śambho trinetrāya … vāmanāya virūpāya svapnādhipataye namaḥ
Bīja oṃ hili hili śūlapāṇaye svāhā
Witnesses ≥27: Śaiva Siddhānta, Kaula, Śākta, Pāñcarātra
Source Varāhamihira, Bṛhadyātrā 16.4–7 (6th c.) → Piṅgalāmata (MS c. 1175) → all tantric witnesses
Narrative Kathāsaritsāgara 1.6.137–138 (Kashmir c. 1070): rite performed, a kumāra appears in the dream
South Adyar TR 470-e Svapnaprakāśikā = Bṛhadyātrā 16.4–7 verbatim; copy dated 1689

Buddhist parallel

Śaiva Buddhist Relation
Dream signs by watch of night and humour: Bṛhadyātrā 16; AV-pariśiṣṭa 68; Brahmayāmala Mañjuśrīmūlakalpa 15.1–52 shared substrate
Niśvāsa Guhyasūtra 3 incubation MMK 15 svapnaṃ me niveda yathābhūtam; Guhyasūtra ↔ MMK formulae (Goodall) same early layer
Adhivāsana dream test before dīkṣā Dīpaṃkarabhadra, Guhyasamājamaṇḍalavidhi 210–213; Saṃvarodaya 18.22; Kriyāsaṃgraha; Sarvadurgatipariśodhana same test before abhiṣeka
Dīkṣāpaddhati: ehi ehi śiśurūpāya … svapnaṃ kathaya MMK: Mañjuśrī kumārarūpin, oṃ he he kumārarūpisvarūpiṇe … phaṭ svāhā same boy-deity, same mantra grammar
