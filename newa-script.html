Notes

  • Breathy consonants (ṅha, ñha, nha, mha, rha, lha) are distinctive to Newar — not found in Devanagari.
  • Retroflex sounds (ṭ, ṭh, ḍ, ḍh, ṇ) occur mainly in Sanskrit loanwords.
  • Each consonant carries an inherent a vowel.

About Newa Script

  • Used from the 10th century in the Kathmandu Valley for Nepal Bhasa (Newari)
  • Derived from Brahmi via the Licchavi and later Nepalese scripts
  • Added to Unicode in 2016 (version 9.0)
  • Also used for Sanskrit, Pali, and Buddhist texts
